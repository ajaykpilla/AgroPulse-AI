AgroPulse is a vertically integrated, technology-driven agritech platform designed to eliminate intermediary markups, mitigate climate vulnerability, and ensure zero-waste farm-to-doorstep distribution.
​By unifying field-level AI crop diagnostics with predictive climate analytics and direct urban quick-commerce demand mapping, AgroPulse empowers Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and smallholder farmers with predictable margins, immediate liquidity, and climate resilience.
​🔥 Key Features
​1. 🧬 Multimodal AI Crop & Soil Health Diagnostics
​Real-time computer vision analysis for field leaf scans and soil sample images.
​Instant detection of crop diseases, pests, and nutrient deficiencies.
​Prescriptive, localized treatment plans favoring biological and organic remedies.
​2. 🌤️ 50-Year Climate Risk & Crop Recommendation Engine
​Granular regional risk mapping leveraging historical weather and micro-climate trends.
​Soil-profile matching to recommend optimal, climate-resilient crop varieties.
​Yield projections and risk mitigation scoring to minimize climate-induced crop failures.
​3. 🛡️ investmentShield Parametric Micro-Insurance
​Automated weather shock detection (drought, unseasonal rainfall, temperature spikes).
​Instant parametric claim settlements to insulate smallholders against capital loss.
​4. 🏬 Smart Godown Liquidity & Quick-Commerce Integration
​Direct linkage between regional FPOs and urban dark store demand sinks.
​Instant credit/advance issuance against digital warehouse receipts for stored produce.
​Dynamic quota allocation ensuring zero post-harvest waste and optimal price discovery.
